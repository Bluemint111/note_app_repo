# AI Reflection

## How AI helped
AI assistance accelerated the setup of the project structure, helped troubleshoot the launcher issues on Windows, and assisted with the frontend microphone and deployment compatibility work.

## Where AI added the most value
- Rapid debugging of startup errors and CORS problems
- Recommending deployment-safe configuration for Render and Vercel
- Helping structure docs, architecture notes, and test planning

## What was changed after AI suggestions
- Updated the React app to use environment-based API and export URLs
- Added root-level startup and stop scripts
- Hardened backend CORS for local and Vercel deployments
- Repaired frontend rendering issues introduced during earlier edits

## Overall assessment
The AI workflow was most valuable for iteration speed and cross-system debugging. The final build is stronger because AI helped identify environment-specific issues quickly, but the final decisions and correctness checks still needed human review.
